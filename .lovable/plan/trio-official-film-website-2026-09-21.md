# TRIO Official Film Website

## Scope

- Replace the placeholder with one continuous cinematic film experience.
- Centralize all editable film content and media references.
- Build the intro, navigation, hero, story, world, characters, production, gallery, vision, creators, trailer, credits, final message, and footer.
- Add responsive, keyboard-accessible interactions and reduced-motion behavior.
- Verify desktop and mobile presentation.

## Design direction

- Near-black theatrical presentation with charcoal, steel, muted ivory, restrained warning crimson, and fire amber.
- Condensed uppercase display typography paired with a clean supporting sans-serif.
- Editorial image placeholders that are clearly marked for official TRIO assets.
- Slow reveal, haze, grain, scan-line, parallax, and restrained system-glitch details.

## Content architecture

- Keep all frequently changed titles, copy, links, credits, people, media, and CTA labels in one typed configuration file.
- Use reusable section, media, card, gallery, modal, and navigation components.
- Create organized public asset folders for final posters, characters, behind-the-scenes, world, logos, video, audio, and icons.

## Validation

- Confirm intro skip, navigation, mobile overlay, tap-reveal world cards, gallery lightbox, trailer modal, keyboard controls, and reduced-motion support.
- Check the full page at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Add route-specific title, description, Open Graph, Twitter card, canonical, and film structured data.
